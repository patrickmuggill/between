Original assets generated with the built-in GPT image-generation tool on November 11, 2026. The tool does not expose a selectable model version. Production files were visually reviewed, resized or encoded with ImageMagick, and retain the original composition.

- `logo.png`: 256 × 256, transparent PNG. Prompt: a refined minimal forest-green mark for Between, a quiet local writing and reference app; two softly organic opposing page/leaf silhouettes with a narrow negative-space opening; readable at 24–32 pixels; no lettering, mockup, or background.
- `walden-pond.webp`: 1920 × 819, WebP quality 88. Prompt: an original premium watercolor picture-book illustration of Walden Pond, Massachusetts, with calm reflective water and a humble wooden cabin close to shore among eastern white pines and deciduous trees; soft morning mist, moss and sage greens, warm ochre wood, translucent pigment washes and handmade paper texture; expansive quiet composition, no people, text, or UI.

The Walden scene is an artistic interpretation, not a documentary depiction of the historical cabin site.
